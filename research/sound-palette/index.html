My suggested starting palette

Mbira, marimba, glass, soft vibraphone, harp, and bongos give us distinct attacks and warm overlapping tails. Add a restrained FM bell or pad later. Start with the CC0 instruments for a reusable sound bank; the extracted Harmony Bloom recordings give us the exact reference material you asked for.

The pattern uses the same eight pitches for the pitched instruments, a short gate and release, and optional shared reverb. It is an audition arrangement, not a recreation of Harmony Bloom’s complete presets. Raw recording players preserve the extracted or downloaded audio. Mario Paint’s native staff labels do not always equal sounding concert pitch.
