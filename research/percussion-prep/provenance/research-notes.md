# Selection evidence

Sources were checked live on October 14, 2026. Licenses and source README files are saved in `../licenses/`.

- VCSL publisher: https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/
- VCSL source and license: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e
- Virtuosity publisher: https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/
- Virtuosity source and license: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums/tree/9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc

Both publishers explicitly identify the sample libraries as CC0. The full exact upstream license text is retained. Per-recording URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in the selection and audio manifests; raw recordings were preserved and verified.

VCSL supplies same-velocity round robins for high/low bongos and closed hats, plus two open-hat accent takes. The earlier Bongo Planet sound-palette preparation pointed to this source; the selected files and rights were independently rechecked for this pack.

VCSL concert bass drums were considered. The selected replacement uses the actual kit kick from Virtuosity, whose close kick microphone and snares-off articulation are a more direct source fit for this role. A close center-hit snare from the same kit is used for the backbeat. Two neighboring snare velocity layers were selected; the manifest explicitly distinguishes these from true same-layer round robins.

VCSL modern snares and Virtuosity mid-microphone hats were also inspected. The chosen close snare has a shorter dominant tail, while VCSL's hat selection avoids importing the much longer Virtuosity open-hat recordings. No claim of artistic superiority is made from waveform measurements alone.

Full upstream tree snapshots, candidate lists, and comparative audio measurements remain locally in this provenance folder. Unselected candidate recordings and the large tree snapshots are excluded from the portable asset ZIP. Only the 12 selected originals and their prepared copies are shipped in that ZIP.
