# Rubber Staircase — Bongo Build

A loping, syncopated kick supports a conversation between low and high bongos. Three differently timed return lanes turn that conversation into a rolling hand-drum cascade, then leave the original pocket intact.

Five-, six- and eight-hop circuits meet at one three-way fork. They enter after three bars, add offset copies to the rhythm, and stop at an 18-hop limit before the relaxed groove returns.

100 BPM; 32 beats / 19.2 seconds per loop. 2 finite generator(s), 47 sounding piece(s), 90 active routed junctions, 91 directed gates.

Choose **Load preset → Drum Loops → Rubber Staircase — Bongo Build**, then **Play**. Preset loading starts paused and keeps the current look. To bring in its coordinated Lilac dusk appearance too, import `Bongo-Planet.json` in the Composition section.

`preview.wav` is a 48 kHz stereo loop from Bongo Planet’s actual engine and current instrument recordings, with settled natural tails. `validation.json` records eight-cycle event and route stability, all reached instruments, bounded work and repeat-matched PCM. Offline validation does not claim physical listening or venue spatial-audio qualification.

Recreate this collection with `npm run songs:drum-builds`.
