# Bongo Planet theme artwork

The approved lineup is Original, Nebula Space and Firefly Garden. Nebula Space keeps its colors and nighttime lighting, with new small 3D ribbon nebulae and eyed stars twenty world units behind the globe. Their softened colors, small scale and zoom-independent framing communicate distance. **Soft background focus** blurs only the distant layer; instruments, globe and sparks stay sharp. `SceneryFocus.ts` owns the reusable color target and blur/composite shader, with musical silhouette clearance applied after the blur. The old cloud characters and little planets remain hidden. Deep Space, Sunday Picnic and Cloud Pudding are archived outside the picker; see [the archive record](../../archives/themes/2026-10-15/README.md).

Archived scenery is built from real Three.js meshes in `src/view/art/ThemeScenery.ts`. Sunday Picnic and Cloud Pudding share puffy cloud and rounded hill characters; the former Nebula Space has colorful nebulae, little planets (including one with a ring) and beveled star characters. The scenery reuses `createToyMaterial` and `createToyEyes` from the instrument meshes. Shared geometry and reusable materials keep historical file imports bounded. Scenery never enters the musical scene or picking targets.

`SceneryMask.ts` gives instruments foreground priority. It renders their actual silhouettes (including faint rear notes and placement ghosts) into one reusable half-resolution target. Scenery materials sample that mask with a small soft margin, so rear-note transparency does not reveal a cloud or star through the note. The scenery also occupies a distant camera-relative depth beyond all musical bounds. The mask follows camera zoom, viewport size and pixel density, and its target/material are disposed with the view.

`cartoon-nebula.svg` and `toy-hills.svg` preserve the previous original vector artwork as historical studies. They are no longer bundled with the game.

`eagle-nebula-v2.png` is retained as historical artwork from the previous revision and is no longer bundled. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 15 October 2026.

Generation prompt:

> Create a wide landscape 16:9 background artwork for a miniature musical toy game. Abstract Eagle Nebula / Pillars of Creation inspired cosmic dust and luminous gas, with jewel-like rainbow color: deep teal and cyan clouds, violet and magenta veils, amber and soft gold illuminated edges on a few irregular sculptural dust pillars. Rich layered depth, beautiful organic wispy detail, dreamy slightly painterly astronomy aesthetic. Dark navy and indigo space remains visible between clouds, especially a substantially darker near-black navy lower third. Keep the middle 50 percent lower contrast and relatively open because a large round toy planet will sit over it; the most interesting pillar silhouettes and bright cyan/magenta/gold wisps appear toward the upper right and outer edges. Sparse tiny distant stars of varying brightness. This is only the background artwork, no planet, no characters, no music instruments, no foreground objects, no text, no UI, no logos. Colors should feel luminous against a dark sky, avoid broad white patches, rainbow stripes, lens flares or a central exploding star. The image should fill the entire frame seamlessly without a border.

`catalog-v1.json`, `catalog-v2.json`, `catalog-v3.json` and `catalog-v4.json` preserve the initial, first sky-revision, final scenery-study and scenery-free curated preset appearances. They recognize untouched browser presets during refresh; v3 also retains all five complete pre-curation designs exactly. They do not replace the approved catalog, captured Original, or explicit imported file values.
