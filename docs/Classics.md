# Bongo Planet — 20 classics

In **Presets**, **Pachelbel’s Canon** and **Toy Ensemble** are the first two songs, in Favorites. Dividers separate [Reworked covers](Reworked.md), the 20 **New classics**, the four [Video game themes](Video-Game-Themes.md), and **More presets**, containing the other existing examples. Select a song and press **Play**. Selecting a preset starts paused and keeps your current look.

These are compact instrumental arrangements of recognizable hooks and opening phrases: six pop, six rock, five classical, and three jazz. Each occupies **82–88 of the 92 nodes**. The music uses actual bass motion and chord tones for its selected passage, with the hook kept louder than the accompaniment. Bass-led songs put their hook in the lower registers. Classical arrangements use softer percussion and longer overlapping notes.

All music uses the current shared sixteenth-note grid and finite directed routes. The opening triplets of Moonlight Sonata are three adjacent hops per group, and In the Mood uses a 3:1 long-short pulse. These are short loop adaptations, not full recordings or complete scores. The music uses one diatonic pitch collection per planet; the selected passages fit that collection, including the parent scales for the modal rock riffs.

**Let It Be.** The familiar chorus on piano, supported by the Am–G–F–C descent and soft sustained glass.

**Stand by Me.** The walking bass hook takes the lead, with warm upper chords and restrained hand percussion.

**Billie Jean.** The low F-sharp bass ostinato stays out in front; short minor-chord answers and tight drums surround it.

**Take On Me.** The quick keyboard hook becomes a clean marimba line, with B-minor, E, A, and D underneath.

**Lean on Me.** A direct, singable piano staircase, with gospel-like chord answers and a low plucked pulse.

**Imagine.** The opening vocal phrase on piano, with the quiet C-to-F motion and a soft glass halo.

**Seven Nation Army.** The seven-note guitar riff anchors the planet, with a grounded beat and dark, sparse chord replies.

**Another One Bites the Dust.** The bass riff keeps its stops and short notes; dry kalimba chords and a clipped backbeat leave it room.

**Sweet Child O’ Mine.** The wide, octave-jumping guitar introduction spans the sphere, with the original lowered tuning and clear bass changes.

**Clocks.** The descending piano figure follows E-flat into B-flat minor, with broad low roots and quiet upper chord tones.

**Nothing Else Matters.** The open-string E-minor arpeggio rolls in six, with long piano roots and very light glass answers.

**(I Can’t Get No) Satisfaction.** The insistent B–C-sharp–D guitar hook against an E dominant vamp, with clipped chord stabs and a firm beat.

**Prelude in C Major.** The first two rolling figures from BWV 846, with a held C foundation and chord tones that follow the arpeggio.

**Minuet in G.** The familiar opening from the Anna Magdalena notebook, with a lilting three-beat accompaniment.

**Eine kleine Nachtmusik.** The punchy opening fanfare, with short articulations, alternating bass, and answering plucked chords.

**Moonlight Sonata.** The opening C-sharp-minor triplet figure over its descending bass, with sustained glass and almost whispered percussion.

**Swan Lake.** The sweeping minor-key swan theme on glass, with a broad bass and gently moving guitar harmony.

**Fly Me to the Moon.** The descending jazz-standard melody, with seventh-chord voicings, soft guitar bass, and offbeat comping.

**Autumn Leaves.** The falling sequence moves through D minor, G, C, and F, with low piano and warm glass sevenths.

**In the Mood.** The famous rising and falling riff gets a 3:1 swing pulse, a walking foundation, and short guitar chords.

The [first 20-song collection](Songbook.md) remains available. Individual files in **public/songs/** include music and the default look; use **Import** to load one, or **Export** to keep your edited version. Regenerate this collection with **npm run songs:classics**. Data and arrangement rules are in **src/core/classics.ts**. Machine tests check every occupied sound node, hook pitch and rhythm, bounded playback, portable imports, menu order, and live browser audio; musical preference remains a listening judgment.
