# Toy Ensemble — chord arrangement

A playful two-generator conversation between all five melodic toys.

Two finite lanes trade Cmaj7, Am7, Dm7 and G7 voicings, with the second lane entering four beats later. Marimba and kalimba answers dance around soft hand percussion.

Choose **Load preset → Favorites - Chords → Toy Ensemble**, then **Play**. Loading a preset starts paused and keeps your current look. Import `Bongo-Planet.json` to load the complete music and its coordinated look.

Sounding locations are distributed around the sphere. The maximum measured gap at the 92 junction probes is 23.8 degrees. Authored spatial paths preserve the original musical timing; selected sparse arrangements include six quiet harmonic replies.

This is an instrumental loop arrangement: 8 master-grid beats at 100 BPM (4.8 seconds), with 8 chord stacks and 2 finite spark sources. Each adjacent hop is one sixteenth note; simultaneous stack layers have independent voices without spawning more sparks.
