# Sea Glass

E-minor-nine colors slowly refract through glass, piano, guitar and small wooden plucks. Quiet duplicates become echoes as different-length routes reunite.

A full-globe finite walk with two forward shortcuts. Each fork sends a long and a short copy into the same later passage, creating routed delays without a growing circuit.

48 BPM; 24 beats / 30 seconds per loop. 1 finite generator(s), 35 sounding piece(s), 92 active routed junctions, 93 directed gates.

Choose **Load preset → Airy & Ambient → Sea Glass**, then **Play**. Preset loading starts paused and keeps the current look. To bring in its coordinated Lilac dusk appearance too, import `Bongo-Planet.json` in the Composition section.

`preview.wav` is a 48 kHz stereo loop from Bongo Planet’s actual engine and current instrument recordings, with settled natural tails. `validation.json` records eight-cycle event and route stability, all reached instruments, bounded work and repeat-matched PCM. Offline validation does not claim physical listening or venue spatial-audio qualification.
