How the planet works

Follow the sparks. Generators send sparks along enabled arrows. Instruments play when a spark arrives. Click arrows to open or close routes.

Build a rhythm. Each hop takes one sixteenth note. Empty junctions make rests; stack up to four instruments at a junction for a chord.

Shape the loop. Tempo sets the speed. Spark interval controls new launches; Spark travel limits their length. Start with finite travel.

02

Write a composition

  1. Describe your idea.

    Try “a slow bongo groove with kalimba and a purple planet.”

  2. Copy your prompt to an AI assistant.

    The builder includes the musical rules and file format. Ask for Bongo-Planet.json.

  3. Import and play.

    Choose Composition → Import, select the file, then press Play.

For another revision, Export your composition and give it to your assistant with your changes.
