The failed predicate combines latest `status.running`, observer error and recorder progress; it did not save its failed status, so the exact false operand cannot be established from that attempt.

Read-only source inspection shows that product event batches and status batches are delivered independently. An emission can arrive before the next status update, which is limited to 20 Hz. Checking the latest running status immediately after an emission therefore creates a plausible startup race. This diagnosis does not establish an audio product fault or qualify any timing gate.
