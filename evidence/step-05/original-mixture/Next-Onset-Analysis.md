# Proposed next onset analysis of the retained original mixture

**Preparation only; no execution or acceptance change.** The practical next step is an independently checked, continuous-parameter timing diagnostic over the existing binary capture. Do not rerun acquisition, alter fixtures, fit a global latency/epoch, or replace an earlier result. Whether at least 120 groups are identifiable must be established by this analysis; 468 modeled groups alone do not guarantee that outcome.

## Requirement and evidence boundary

[Rules.md](../../../Rules.md) requires actual loopback onsets against independent analytic expectations, removal of only measured fixed output latency, at least 120 onsets across F02/F03, F06 and F08 including 100/250 ms main-thread stalls, absolute-error median ≤5 ms, p95 ≤10 ms and maximum ≤20 ms, with no missing/duplicate ordinary events. Scheduled receipts and offline PCM cannot substitute. Count simultaneous audible groups once here; do not inflate the denominator with coincident voices.

The rule does **not** require recovering a generator/node identity uniquely from mono PCM under every conceivable alternative implementation. The completed logical ledger independently checks expected IDs, sounds and trigger/onset counts; actual audio must separately establish audible entrances and expose distinguishable omissions/extra sound. A voice intentionally stolen before sounding is not a missing ordinary audible event. Conversely, a logical receipt is not proof that its voice reached the output.

Mono capture cannot distinguish exchanging identical voice IDs, or a duplicated batch that evicts the original identical batch before either sounds. Saturation and destructive combination can remove further information. Preserve such ambiguities explicitly. Do not require an impossible universal mono-identity proof, but do not claim PCM excluded these alternatives. A complete gate decision must state the combined logical/audio evidence and this limit; this proposed diagnostic always leaves `qualificationPassed:false` for subsequent review.

## Measurement model and continuous coverage

1. Retain pre-entrance samples, attack and sufficient following mixture for each group, with neighboring voices and ownership. Propose **RMS tolerance1e-6**, as used in the existing analyzer's counterexample tests, as this new diagnostic's explicitly predeclared model-family compatibility criterion. Retain maximum residual and the original strict RMS-plus-maximum fidelity decisions alongside it; those F06/F08 failures remain failures. If the local nominal family has no compatible member, label model mismatch rather than increasing tolerance. Long-window RMS alone must not establish attack presence: also retain attack-local residual/contrast and explicit withheld-attack alternatives so a short missing attack cannot disappear in a long quiet denominator.
2. Estimate **phase/envelope origin and admission independently**. Include translated origin, unchanged-phase late insertion, their joint combinations, missing group, and duplicated group. Entrance is the later of phase origin and actual modeled admission, only if the voice survives to sound. Recompute capacity/ownership for every candidate; do not reuse nominal ownership as the old cursor diagnostic deliberately did. Include independently delayed/missing audible waveform signatures within a chord/batch; a common group shift alone cannot show that every distinct component starts together. Identical signatures remain a multiplicity/identity limitation.
3. Replace sampled-grid half-step padding with certified interval exclusion. Admission is enumerated at candidate render-quantum boundaries. Split continuous origin intervals at onset-order, duration and ownership discontinuities; within a fixed branch, bound sine/kick/envelope contributions and forward `tanh` with outward numerical allowances. A small subset of discriminating samples can give a conservative lower bound on full-window squared error and reject a cell cheaply. Only surviving cells receive a complete original-sample comparison. Interval widths must follow proven residual bounds, not an assumed smooth interpolation between grid points. If bounds remain loose, the group is unresolved.
4. Search beyond the desired ±20 ms result. Explicitly cover the actual100 ms negative, individual carrier-period aliases and adjacent fixture cycles. A finite local window cannot exclude arbitrary shifts beyond it: either extend comparison/search through the entire retained segment with full relevant prior/tail ownership, or report the remaining outside-domain ambiguity. Unchanged steady tails are not entrance witnesses. Any compatible far-away interval, edge-touching solution or uncovered ownership branch prevents a bounded timing claim. Multiple neighboring groups can exchange/cancel acoustic effects; cases requiring a joint ambiguity search must be labeled conditional or unresolved, not certified by one-at-a-time fits alone.
5. Keep numerical-clock models explicit. The original ordinal-time model and declared block-cursor hypothesis can be separate sensitivity analyses; never minimize across them and retain only the favorable result. The full-cursor result gives exact F06 and close F08 waveform agreement, but its earlier history and command floor are unobserved, and it holds nominal ownership fixed. It is therefore not automatically a calibrated production-clock oracle. Report whether timing bounds are stable across declared models and what model assumption each bound requires. No fitted clock curve may absorb an onset delay.

For an identified group retain the entire compatible entrance interval in output-sample time, its expected analytic time, signed error interval, explicit calibration/sample allowance, acoustic alternatives tested, and unexcluded branches. Compute conservative absolute-error median/p95/max from per-group upper bounds with a predeclared percentile convention. The independently stated one-sample calibration quantization allowance is not a measured physical presentation bound; do not invent a smaller uncertainty. Require ≥120 identified groups with the stated fixture/stall coverage before reporting even a conditional timing subset. Missing/duplicate and unresolved denominators remain visible independently of percentile results.

## Controls, implementation and stopping rule

Prepare a new pure interval-bound module, a bounded sequential runner and independent scalar counterexamples; keep the frozen oracle/analyzer/results unchanged. Controls must include off-grid origins, joint translation/late admission, a delayed single chord component, weak/saturated attack, periodic aliases outside20 ms, adjacent-group ambiguity, missing/extra groups, identical-batch replacement, erased voices, search-boundary escape and the actual100 ms negative. Numerical-bound controls need independently calculated enclosures, including rounding and branch boundaries; fine-grid agreement is not proof of a continuous bound.

Keep **120 seconds and256 MiB** as detected execution limits, with a final guard and a new exclusive output. Stream one segment/group at a time; bound the interval frontier, candidate count and retained detail bytes before execution. Cache immutable background contributions only where ownership is proven unchanged. Do not rerender every long dense segment for every grid point or retain full event objects. Record actual work counts, cell widths and resource checks. On exhaustion preserve the completed prefix and unresolved remainder, exit nonzero, and stop without automatic retry/cap increase. No claim is made that certified joint searches for all468 groups will fit this budget.

The first deliverable is thus a feasibility/certification report, potentially with≥120 bounded acoustic group timings. If continuous/alias coverage or mixture identifiability cannot establish that denominator, the precise result is that this retained recording supports fewer identified groups or only model-conditional bounds. The live-output gate then remains open; it cannot be closed by selecting good fits, using scheduled timestamps, changing the original fixtures after the fact, or relabeling the full-cursor fidelity result as onset measurement.
