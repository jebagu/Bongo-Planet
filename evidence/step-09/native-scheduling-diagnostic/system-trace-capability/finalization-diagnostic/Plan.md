# Bongo Planet — idle collector finalization diagnostic

Preparation status was source-only. Root and reviewer subsequently authorized exactly one invocation; **attempt-01 completed with failure** after the original collector-command budget was exceeded. No sample or export ran. See `Result.md` and retained raw attempt artifacts. Existing earlier capability source, failed partial trace and log evidence are preserved. No repeat invocation is authorized.

The first capability attempt exceeded its configured 45-second collector-command budget. Its logs show internal activity during saving; that does not establish a deadlock, permission cause or successful finalization. This experiment measures when phases and work occur before proposing a collector-budget change.

Run, only after GO: `python3 scripts/system-trace-finalization-diagnostic.py run`. Its exclusive `finalization-diagnostic/attempt-01/` directory prevents a second invocation after success or failure. Scope is one self-owned idle Node process and idle named Worker, one five-second System Trace attached to that Node PID, and at most one intrusive collector-only sample. Node's `threadId` is not presented as a Mach thread identifier. Product audio, browser, receiver and ambient PIDs are untouched.

The collector command remains `xcrun xctrace record --template 'System Trace' --attach <owned-node-pid> --time-limit 5s --output <new-attempt>/raw.trace`. The 45-second limit starts at collector launch, not at an inferred saving start. At expiry, retain failure and signal the owned collector with SIGINT, then SIGTERM at 55 seconds and SIGKILL at 58 seconds if needed. No recording or export retry, sudo, privacy bypass or settings change is implemented.

Stdout/stderr chunks receive UTC and monotonic elapsed timestamps as they are drained; first recognizable recording/completion/saving transitions are retained separately. This is observation time with up to a polling interval of delay, not an internal collector timestamp. Every two seconds retain only owned PID lineage, PID/start identities, process state and metadata-only trace file count/summed size. Walks stop at 2,048 entries or 50ms and mark truncation. Growth proves observed activity only. Raw process arguments for unrelated processes are not retained.

At the first loop reaching collector elapsed 20 seconds, launch exactly one `/usr/bin/sample <owned-collector-pid> 1 1 -file <new-attempt>/collector-sample.txt` only if saving has already been recognized, the collector is still active, and a fresh scoped process inventory confirms its PID/start identity and xctrace/xcrun executable name. Otherwise record a skipped sample and do not sample later. The sample has a one-second requested duration and a four-second command limit. Permission/tool errors remain failures with raw output; do not bypass or retry. Sampling may perturb idle finalization and cannot qualify musical scheduling.

Limits: 75-second total wall budget, with seven seconds reserved for cleanup; 256KiB drained output per owned process, 1MiB event metadata, 38 metadata observations, 1MiB transient process inventory, and a detected 2MiB sample-file bound. Because `sample` writes its own file, its byte bound is observed every loop and can overshoot before termination; any overshoot is reported with the original file retained. Event-cap exhaustion marks truncation and continues cleanup. Final trace size is intentionally not capped by deleting/truncating original collector output.

Cleanup inventories and signals only direct owned children or previously observed descendants whose current PID/start identity still matches. It reports direct-child exit separately from remaining observed descendants and inventory failure. Unobserved detached service helpers cannot be proven absent by this inventory; no blanket all-helper cleanup claim is permitted. If ownership or terminal collection cannot be confirmed, report the blocker to root before any next experiment. No ambient process/group cleanup is implemented.

Source-only cleanup review: the sample uses one absolute launch deadline (`sample_start + 4`) across both the active-collector loop and collector-exit transition. Its remaining wait never resets to four seconds. Logging I/O failure is latched and cannot prevent owned-child cleanup; children are registered immediately after spawn, before pipe setup. Direct-child signal errors remain explicit and do not skip other owned children. Final output is drained after exit, and idle Node exit0 plus Worker exit0 acknowledgment are required. The trace metadata walk also checks its time budget on each directory, including empty directories. These are source changes, not executed failure-injection checks.

Artifacts: exact diagnostic/idle source copies, invocation, exact launch arguments/PIDs, incremental `events.jsonl`, raw or partial `raw.trace`, optional raw collector sample, bounded cleanup/result metadata. Existing local version/template inventory is reference evidence, not a fresh execution-time version check; actual Node version is reported by the owned idle process. This diagnostic does not export scheduler rows, establish Mach worker mapping, change deadline policy or qualify the product.

Post-terminal readback is complete in `Review.md` and `attempt-01/retention-manifest.json`; all274 retained regular trace files have streaming SHA256 bindings.
