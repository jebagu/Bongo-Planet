## Independent deadlines and optional sample

The parent owns an absolute monotonic110second acquisition target independently of the child event loop, followed by at most15seconds of budgeted cleanup/inspection, giving a125second outer target from fork initiation. Parent timers themselves are cooperative and subject to host scheduling: this is not a real-time guarantee. Decisions use fresh elapsed monotonic time when the parent resumes, so delayed callbacks do not reopen acquisition or renew wait budgets. Completion requires a validated terminal acknowledgment, child exit0 and confirmed owned resource closure. At deadline or any protocol/ownership failure it marks failure, snapshots retained phases and applies bounded TERM then KILL only to verified owned processes/group. Forced termination never passes. The parent checks observed Chrome/renderers, owned Node group, TCP5424 and UDP5425 closure and retains exact command/output/exit for those checks. Closure is scoped to observed/identified resources; unobserved detached helpers are not claimed absent.

An optional future `--sample-on-stall` permits at most one `/usr/bin/sample <exact-child-pid> 1 1 -file /dev/stdout` after15seconds without structural phase progress, only after fresh PID/command/incarnation ownership confirmation. After recording the observed failure or unknown closure, the supervisor separately disconnects its owned IPC, destroys its parent-side pipes, and unreferences every owned child/inspection/sample process handle.
