## Required freshness mechanism

A recent point and a narrow read bracket bound the offset at that read; they do not, by themselves, bound an unseen clock step109ms later. Observed historical slope, a100ms nominal timer, or a hardcoded allowance cannot fill this gap. Pair age computed only from UTC values can also be masked by a UTC adjustment. An implementable current-pair repair must therefore choose and validate one explicit mechanism:

- A declared independent bound on UTC/performance offset change over the measured monotonic age, including forward/backward steps and cross-process clock agreement. Every bracket or age that makes the bound exceed the unchanged gates fails before execution. This is the smallest runtime change, but the existing evidence does not establish that bound; observed maxima alone are insufficient.
- Independent continuous monotonic/UTC bracket recording at sufficiently fine actual intervals. Match a callback's observed UTC value to the recorded monotonic interval, retain the bracketing rows, and use the full interval width as uncertainty. UTC reversals, repeated ambiguous values, missing bracketing data, or scheduler gaps widen or fail. A dedicated clock recorder can isolate ordinary Node-main-thread stalls, while shared host descheduling still widens the actual interval and fails. This is measurement instrumentation, not another deadline scheduler. Retrospective receipt bracketing alone does not authorize an unbounded provisional timestamp at execution; admission/clock readiness must provide a contemporaneous bound, and later evidence must independently confirm it.

The second mechanism offers a path to measured age bounds without inventing a slew-rate constant, but is a larger protocol change. If neither mechanism supplies a contemporaneous bound, keep failure rather than claim that a current-point substitution fixes qualification. A direct independently calibrated monotonic render-time witness could avoid UTC entirely, but its API availability, causal timing semantics and uncertainty would require a separate bounded diagnostic; a hardware presentation estimate is not automatically a process-execution witness.

Clock metadata must become current before the corrected per-quantum guard, without applying buffered producer heartbeat/start commands first. Ordered pair identities must reject stale updates; a fresh pair cannot retarget pending controls or revive expired epochs. Retain raw frame continuity, actual sample writes, cancellation ordering, bounded receipt backlog and watchdog checks.

Required controlled regressions: positive UTC change corrects observed time without altering targetFrame; negative residual can worsen and still trips its guard; clock change between sample and callback widens/fails instead of fabricating freshness; a wide bracket widens/fails; stale/out-of-order pairs cannot replace newer pairs; unchanged deadlines, cancel/epoch/heartbeat behavior survive metadata updates. Only after those bounds are reviewed and the new model passes isolated tests should a new immutable-source F01/F04 capture start.
