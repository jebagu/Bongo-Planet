## Addendum — tagged Chromium accounting supports the conditional continuity argument

Root subsequently retained the public Chromium155.0.8059.39 DOMWebSocket source and retrieval provenance under `chromium-readback/`, matching installed version metadata. I read its relevant accounting branches: open-state sends increment `buffered_amount_`; sends after closure increment `buffered_amount_after_close_`; the getter returns their sum. Consumption is subtracted through posted/context callbacks, not added by incoming traffic. The retained source is26,824 bytes, SHA `4bf9a49ad6d6f433bbeb016e10335d1a6bfdbbc53212cc97bf028e8792d2203c`, from the Chromium public tag URL recorded in Retrieval.json.

Under conformance to that tagged implementation and ordinary non-reentrant execution of the observed synchronous producer callback, a low production read followed by a high callback exit requires an intervening same-socket send. The first such send exposes a low before-send value; with no send, the exit stays low. Thus checking all retained confirmed-producer entry/exit and same-socket before-send reads supplies a defensible conditional guard against an unseen reset. Source01 does not yet perform those checks, so B02 remains a blocker for it. This supports fixing the existing observations rather than adding a getter interceptor. Public tag/version agreement is not a reproducible installed-binary attestation, and discrete observations still do not certify continuously measured physical/kernel behavior.
